# Prepare evidence for normal native reviews

Keep evidence capture, prelaunch validation and result freshness behind one
module, while the orchestrator supplies the prepared material to its normal
native reviewer. An SDK-backed launcher was tried but introduced a separate
model-drift blocker without improving the ordinary review path; its unshipped
source and observations are retained in session artifacts. The approved revision
uses instruction-coordinated native handoff rather than claiming universal
interception or independent runtime-model attestation, and preserves review-stage
ownership, read-only native roles and skill-provider independence.
