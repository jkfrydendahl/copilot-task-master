# Workbench Review Dispatch

The vocabulary for reviews dispatched by the workbench. Review scope and its
evidence are distinct from the surrounding workspace.

## Language

**Review scope**:
The task-owned changes and requirements selected for a review.

**Review packet**:
The collected evidence describing a review scope at a particular repository
state.

**Workspace inventory**:
The complete accounting of repository changes, including changes outside the
review scope.

**Stale review**:
A review whose evidence is no longer current.

**Review stage**:
The delivery stage that owns review scope, review axes, reviewer instructions and
the interpretation of findings.

**Review dispatch**:
The assignment of a review scope and its evidence to a selected reviewer.

**Review handoff**:
The evidence and instructions supplied to a selected reviewer before it starts.
